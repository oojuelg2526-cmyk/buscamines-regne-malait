Game Concept

What is the Cursed Kingdom?

Buscamines del Regne Malaït is a single-player puzzle game designed for mobile and web. It keeps the beloved logic of classic Minesweeper — reveal safe tiles, read the numeric clues, flag hidden traps — but wraps it in a modern-medieval atmosphere where the board resembles the map of a lost kingdom.

What truly sets it apart is a post-victory decision that turns each cleared board into a moment of genuine drama: take your points and leave safely, or push your luck and double everything — at the risk of losing it all. That risk is what cursed the kingdom. That choice is why it matters.

Key Features

What's Inside the Kingdom

Signature Mechanic

The Cursed Expansion

This is the differential mechanic that gives the game its name. Once a board is cleared, the player faces a crucial decision — and whichever path they take, the stakes are real.

⚔ Option A — Close the Game

  • Keep all earned points securely
  • Session ends immediately and safely
  • Points added to the global ranking
  • No risk — your score is guaranteed

🔥 Option B — Expand the Board

  • New tiles with hidden traps are added
  • Victory → score ×2 (repeatable: ×4, ×8…)
  • Trigger a trap → 0 points total
  • Can be repeated successively for legendary scores
"To take the risk is to curse your own game. That is why this kingdom is lost — and why it tempts every adventurer."
✦

Score System

How Points Are Calculated

The scoring formula rewards both skill and courage. Speed gives you a base edge; expansions multiply everything.

Score = Base Points (difficulty) + Speed Bonus × Risk Multiplier
Situation Multiplier Result
Victory, no risk taken ×1 Base + Speed Bonus
+ 1 Expansion cleared ×2 (Base + Bonus) × 2
+ 2 Expansions cleared ×4 (Base + Bonus) × 4
+ 3 Expansions cleared ×8 (Base + Bonus) × 8 — Legendary
Defeat during expansion — 0 points. Everything lost.
Normal defeat — 0 points.
