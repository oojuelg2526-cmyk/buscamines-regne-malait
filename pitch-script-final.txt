PITCH SCRIPT — Buscamines del Regne Malaït

NOTAS GENERALES (solo para ti)
───────────────────────────────
• Habla despacio. 3 minutos parece poco pero no vayas con prisa.
• No leas las slides — míralas un segundo y luego mira a la profe.
• Haz una pausa breve después de las frases importantes.
• Evita: "kinda", "stuff", "cool", "basically", "so yeah".
• Las frases en inglés están simplificadas para que suenen naturales,
  no como si estuvieras leyendo una redacción.


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SLIDE 1 — PORTADA  (~20 segundos)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

[Mira a la profe, sonríe, empieza con calma]

"Good morning. Thank you for your time today.
My name is Óscar Ojuel, and I am here to pitch
Buscamines del Regne Malaït — a mobile puzzle game
where every victory becomes a choice,
and every choice could cost you everything."

[Pausa de 1 segundo. Deja que el gancho aterrice antes de pasar slide.]


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SLIDE 2 — WHO I AM  (~15 segundos)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

[Esta slide es rápida, no le des mucho tiempo]

"I am a first-year DAW student and the sole developer
behind this project. My focus is on building experiences
that are intuitive, engaging, and worth coming back to."

[Pasa la slide mientras dices la última frase]


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SLIDE 3 — THE CONCEPT  (~20 segundos)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

[Señala brevemente la imagen/mockup del juego]

"So — what is the game? It takes classic Minesweeper,
a game almost everyone already knows,
and wraps it in a medieval atmosphere.
The board becomes the map of a lost kingdom.
Mines become hidden traps.
Numbers become warnings from fallen scouts."

[Pequeña pausa]

"It is mobile-first, single-player,
and built for short replayable sessions."


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SLIDE 4 — GAMEPLAY  (~15 segundos)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

[Esta slide también es rápida — solo explica lo básico]

"The mechanics are simple. A timer runs from the start.
You reveal tiles, flag suspected traps,
and win by uncovering every safe tile.
Trigger a single trap — and the game ends immediately.
No second chances."

[Pausa. Baja un poco la voz para dar peso a la transición]

"But winning is not the end.
That is where things get interesting."


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SLIDE 5 — THE CURSED EXPANSION  (~35 segundos)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

[Esta es la slide más importante — dale tiempo y seguridad]
[Señala las dos columnas mientras explicas cada opción]

"This is the mechanic that gives the game its name."

[Pausa breve]

"After clearing the board, the player has two options.
Option A — secure your points and walk away safely.
Option B — expand the board, add more traps,
and double your score if you survive."

[Señala la columna de riesgo]

"But if you trigger a trap during the expansion,
you lose everything. Every single point."

[Pausa]

"And you can keep expanding — times two, times four,
times eight — each time risking it all again."

[Baja la voz ligeramente para la frase final]

"That risk is what cursed the kingdom.
That choice is why it matters."


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SLIDE 6 — DIFFICULTY & SCORING  (~15 segundos)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

[Señala los tres niveles rápidamente]

"The game offers three difficulty levels —
Easy, Medium, and Hard.
Each has a different board size and base score.
A speed bonus rewards fast play,
and the multiplier system means a Hard board
with three expansions can reach a legendary result."


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SLIDE 7 — LEADERBOARD  (~10 segundos)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

[Rápido — no es slide protagonista]

"All scores feed into a global leaderboard
with two views: your best single-game score,
and your total accumulated points.
No registration needed — your player name is your identity."


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SLIDE 8 — SEE IT IN ACTION  (~15 segundos)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

[Señala cada banner mientras lo nombras]

"These four screens show the core experience:
the live game board, the difficulty selection,
the Cursed Expansion decision,
and the global leaderboard."

[Pequeña pausa]

"The visual identity is consistent throughout —
dark stone textures, gold typography,
and a touch-first layout designed for mobile."


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SLIDE 9 — WHY INVEST?  (~20 segundos)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

[Aquí sube un poco la energía — estás vendiendo el proyecto]

"Minesweeper is one of the most recognised games in history,
which means the learning curve is almost zero.
I have taken that foundation and added something it never had —
a meaningful decision after every win."

[Pausa breve]

"The design is user-centred and mobile-first.
Sessions are short enough for a commute,
but the risk mechanic and leaderboard
create real long-term motivation.
No account, no friction — maximum accessibility."


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SLIDE 10 — THANK YOU  (~15 segundos)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

[Mira directamente a la profe. Habla con calma y confianza]

"To summarise — a familiar mechanic, elevated with atmosphere
and tension. A unique risk-reward decision after every win.
And a user-focused design built for broad appeal."

[Pausa final]

"I am confident this project offers something genuinely different.
I would be delighted to answer any questions you may have.
Thank you."

P: What technologies are you using?
R: "The game is built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript for the
   front end, and a relational SQL database for the leaderboard
   and session tracking."

P: Who is your target audience?
R: "Casual gamers and puzzle lovers — mainly teenagers and adults
   who enjoy short strategic sessions on mobile. The Minesweeper
   base keeps the entry barrier very low."

P: What makes it different from original Minesweeper?
R: "The Cursed Expansion. Original Minesweeper ends when you win.
   This game turns that moment into a new, higher-stakes decision.
   That emotional tension is entirely new to the format."

P: Is the prototype fully functional?
R: "The prototype demonstrates the core visual identity and user
   flows. Full functionality, including the live database and
   leaderboard, is currently in development."
